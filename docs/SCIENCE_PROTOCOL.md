## RQ1 — action-dependent cascading safety

**Sampling**: keep natural, trajectory-log-seeded samples in a separated cohort from deliberately searched critical cases. Before looking at outcomes, fix a maneuver intent, terminal-speed tolerance, ego progress tolerance, acceleration limits and route legality. Reject initial contacts and invalid states. Mark all scenarios generated by adaptive parameter search as `POSTHOC`.

**Positive criterion**: both actions are legal/feasible and ego-contact-free, relative task progress/comfort within declared tolerance; one has a **real geometric non-ego** collision or larger severe near miss in a *separately audited* reactive world; its alternate avoids the third-party failure. Demonstrate the B-mediator exchange, independent negative controls, and sensitivity to reaction delay/braking capacity and initial spacing. Show contact sensor truth vs 0.1s/continuous OBB checks; a tiny TTC difference is insufficient.

The frozen case included in this repo satisfies the criterion **only inside its specified delayed-IDM surrogate**, with B swapped under a structural replay. It does not satisfy a CARLA or field verification criterion.

## RQ2 — decision-critical response discriminability

**Primary measure**: sign error for pairwise downstream union collision risk differences, conditioned on a predeclared minimum actionable difference and matched ego/task constraints. Secondary: false confidence / underresolution at fixed low-fidelity response query budget, event braking onset, policy-conditioned margin error, geometric contact margin, Fréchet partial lower/upper bounds and downstream union error.

**Baselines**: source Monte Carlo, source action-conditioned full rollout, source reuse by geometric distance, CBC without physical terms, CBC without graph propagation, model ensemble variance and source-model disagreement. Compare at identical prediction horizon, scene/candidate set, source reference family and actual measured compute budget.

**Potential-outcome caution**: the mean collision probability contrast is defined under a declared response law. The individual-world flip probability is not identified by marginal logs. Use coupling-free sharp Fréchet bounds and treat paired rollout flips as conditional on explicitly fixed structural seeds.

## RQ3 — decision reliability under limited evidence

Primary outcome is the rate of **wrong accepted actions** under the independent joint downstream event (per accepted scenario and per all scenarios), with abstention/coverage, task efficiency and deadline failures reported together. Compare CBC-only, CBC+DSE, CBC+DSE+TIV, full per-candidate prediction and matched-budget uniform/entropy/model-disagreement/EPG-like querying. Report risk–coverage and task-progress–risk curves rather than only average collision rate.

DSE calibration uses one held-out scene family collection, and source selection/query policy fitting uses separate source-audit data. The final executable-prefix guard is an independently checked mechanic, not a Boolean acceptance flag. Adaptive TIV final release intervals require their own held-out calibration or a valid sequential analysis; the pre-query static quantile is not automatically reusable.

Calibration error in this implementation: for n held-out scenes, let q be the order statistic of each scene's max coordinate deviation between predicted risks and K-audit-world empirical frequencies. The Hoeffding buffer accounts for finite independent audit worlds over the recorded reference mixture and all probability coordinates. Statistical guarantee pertains to the reference family/mix under independent, exchangeable scenes; not each fixed real driver.

**Scene leakage**: 9s WOMD windows from the same longer 20s source segment may share track history. Train/calibration/test should split by parent 20s segment/log before extracting windows. The current scene ID partition is only a *basic exact-identity safeguard*, not a sufficient publication split. For nuPlan split by the entire DB/log (not nearby timestamps). For ReactSim, hold out underlying nuPlan log names, not just individual behavior PKLs. For CARLA, hold out whole actor-parameter seeds and initial-location families.

## Falsification gates

1. If physical 4-car reproduction under a second policy family shows no valid ego-safe/downstream-unsafe matched action pairs, do not assert an observed class of realistic counterexamples.
2. If CBC fails to reduce critical signed ranking error relative to geometry-only and full-response baselines at equal budget, do not assert the boundary module helps RQ2.
3. If calibrated DSE's accepted-action error remains high or coverage collapses to zero under realistic tolerances and deadlines, report that the evidence protocol is insufficient; do not claim successful certification.
4. If source-aware TIV fails to improve truly independent audited decision loss at equal compute, or if query results never reduce final uncertainty, do not claim benefit of targeted verification.
5. If results depend on a hand-picked CARLA scenario or a particular driver set, report mechanism-only evidence and an explicitly conditional scope.

The release package does not assert that these gates have been met.
